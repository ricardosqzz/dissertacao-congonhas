# Congonhas poster presentation notes

## A short presentation

Air quality management plans need to connect emission control with the places receiving pollution. In a mining municipality such as Congonhas, a broad attainment classification can leave much of the map in the same category. Our research asks what additional spatial information can be obtained from the modelled concentration field before translating it into municipal zoning.

We analysed an existing AERMOD scenario using a 2023 emissions inventory and meteorological conditions from 2021 to 2023. The regional network contains 1,497 receptors, including 1,008 within the municipal analytical footprint. We retain all 311 zoning identifiers for reporting.

The first map divides annual PM2.5 concentrations into five relative classes. These quintiles are different from the statistical groups in the second map. That map uses k-means on six standardized indicators: hourly maxima, daily maxima and annual means of both PM10 and PM2.5. Two groups provided the strongest internal separation among the solutions tested for this set of indicators.

The lower maps connect the receiving pattern with local emission density and proposed management roles. The PM2.5 receiving-priority class contains 202 receptors, forming 14 contiguous regions over 52.4 square kilometres and intersecting 137 zoning records. Within this receiving support, Gerdau accounts for 74.7% of the area-weighted modelled concentration, Urban Total for 16.5%, and other groups for 8.9%. These are received concentration shares, rather than shares of locally emitted mass.

Our main contribution is to separate receiving priorities from areas that require source investigation, while retaining municipal zoning as the reporting reference. Spatial neighbourhood tests and observed-modelled discrepancies constrain the interpretation. New emission-timing simulations and independent evaluation are still required before operational control scenarios are adopted.

## Reading the figures

Start with the upper-left quintiles, then compare them with the upper-right joint profiles. Use the lower-left emission-concentration matrix to distinguish local emission pressure from received concentration. The lower-right map translates these combinations into proposed management roles. Grey or non-significant statistical results do not establish clean air. The contribution bars summarize the pooled receiving-priority regions. The sensitivity bars show Jaccard overlap of local high-high receptor sets for alternative neighbourhood sizes.

## Questions to anticipate

The silhouette of 0.481 measures separation between the two statistical groups. It does not measure AERMOD accuracy. The emission-density cutoff of 8.50 t/year/km² is an empirical association with annual concentration above P80, not an allowable emission limit. Urban Total combines urban roads, railways and urban nuclei, including road resuspension and combustion. Health assessment belongs to the broader dissertation and is outside this poster.
